Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Wine Cellar Note ("the App", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to provide the App's features:

CategoryDetails
Account infoYour name and email address, provided through Google Sign-In when you log in.
Wine recordsInformation you enter: wine name, producer, country, grape, vintage, price, purchase place, ratings, tasting notes, tags, and photos you attach.
Wine label photosImages you upload for AI label recognition.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or device identifiers for advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To store and display your personal wine collection.
  • To provide AI features: recognizing wine labels from photos and generating sommelier recommendations and taste reports.
  • To analyze your taste preferences within the App (this analysis is shown only to you).

3. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party providers to operate the App. Your data may be processed by:

Supabase — secure cloud database and storage where your account and wine records are saved. See Supabase Privacy Policy.

OpenAI — when you use AI label recognition or the sommelier chat/report, the relevant text and the wine label image you submit are sent to OpenAI to generate a response. See OpenAI Privacy Policy.

Google — used for sign-in authentication. See Google Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your personal data to any party.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers protected by industry-standard measures. Access to your wine records is restricted to your own account through authenticated, row-level security. While no method of transmission is 100% secure, we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

5. Data Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual wine records at any time within the App. If you wish to delete your entire account and all associated data, contact us (see below) and we will remove it.

6. Your Rights

  • Access — view all your data within the App.
  • Correction — edit any wine record at any time.
  • Deletion — delete records, or request full account deletion.
  • If you are in the EU/EEA (GDPR) or California (CCPA), you have additional rights including data portability and the right to object to processing.

7. Children's Privacy

This App relates to alcoholic beverages and is intended for users of legal drinking age in their country (18 or 21+ depending on jurisdiction). It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision. Significant changes will be communicated within the App.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at:
